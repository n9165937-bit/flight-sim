# Skyward — Flight Simulator

A browser flight sim with 8 airplanes (Skylark trainer, Vortex aerobatic, Kestrel twin, Meridian jet, PC-12, F-16, F/A-18, F-22) and 4 helicopters (Bell 407, UH-60 Black Hawk, AH-64 Apache, CH-47 Chinook), three maps (Green Valley, Red Rock Canyon, Alpine Peaks) with runways and helipads, landing practice with scoring, ring courses, wind, and live multiplayer.

**Play:** https://n9165937-bit.github.io/flight-sim/

## Multiplayer
Open the game, type a pilot name in the menu, and fly. Everyone on the same map sees each other's planes with name tags. Positions are shared through a free public relay (HiveMQ), so there's nothing to set up — just send friends the link.

## Controls
W/S pitch · A/D roll · Q/E rudder · Shift/Z throttle (collective in helicopters) · F/V flaps · G gear · B brakes · K Cobra (fighters) · C camera · R reset · Esc menu

## Weather & settings
- Weather: Clear, Overcast, Rain, Snow, Fog, Sunset, Night (runway lights and stars at night). Change it any time from the pause menu.
- Your aircraft, map, airport, graphics, wind, weather, camera view and sound setting are saved automatically.
